An independent developer studio based in Okinawa, Japan.

We create digital products, including software applications, websites, and games, and thoughtfully craft digital media such as art and video.

Founder & operator

Lena Schulze-Lau holds a Ph.D. in machine learning from OIST, Japan, and has spent the last decade building production AI systems across healthcare, retail, and industrial use cases.

She currently works as a lead AI/ML engineer in the medical tech space in Japan, with past work including leading the team behind UNIQLO’s in-house recommendation engine.

This studio is where she builds outside that lane: indie games, mobile apps, and projects like Folio, a note-taking app for mental wellbeing.
